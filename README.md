# Adidas Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze Adidas sales performance and generate business insights.

## 🎯 Business Problems

- Analyze overall sales, profit, units sold and operating margin.
- Identify high-performing regions and products.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales performance across different sales methods.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business performance monitoring.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Slicers
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
- KPI Analysis

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

- KPI analysis for Total Sales, Total Profit, Units Sold and Average Operating Margin.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Regional sales performance analysis.
- Product sales performance analysis.
- Sales method analysis.
- Interactive filtering using Region, Product, Retailer and Sales Method slicers.

## 💡 Key Insights

- West region recorded the highest sales among the analyzed regions.
- In-store was the leading sales method.
- Sales performance varied across months.
- Product-level analysis helped identify stronger-performing products.
- Overall sales were approximately $900M.
- Total profit was approximately $332.1M.
- Units sold were approximately 2.48M.
- Average operating margin was approximately 42.3%.
